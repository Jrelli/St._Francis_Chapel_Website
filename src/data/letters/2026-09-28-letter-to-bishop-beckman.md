Dear Bishop Mark,

May the Lord give you His peace.

After a prolonged period of prayer, reflection, study, and consultation, having already informed my Bishop Kevin C. Rhoades, as an act of courtesy, I write to inform you of a decision which I know you may find difficult and with which you may strongly disagree.

Following my last conversation with Fr. David Carter, I have decided to associate myself with the Society of Saint Pius X in the capacity it describes as a Priest Friend, and, with its assistance, to continue the exercise of my priestly ministry. I have also chosen to accept the invitation from multiple Catholics of the Diocese of Knoxville to take responsibility for a Traditional Latin Mass Mission Chapel in Dandridge, also affiliated with the SSPX.

Speaking on behalf of the Saint Francis of Assisi Traditional Latin Mass Mission and Una Voce Non-Profit Foundation, we do not take this step lightly, nor do we take it out of contempt for ecclesiastical authority or with any intention of abandoning the Catholic Church. We remain Catholics, and I, a Catholic Priest. We profess the Catholic faith. We recognize the Roman Pontiff as the successor of Saint Peter, we recognize the hierarchical constitution of the Church, and we recognize and respect your episcopal office. We do not intend by this decision to embrace sedevacantism, to establish ourselves outside the Catholic Church, or to refuse ecclesiastical communion. At each and everyone of our Masses we remember the name of the Supreme Pontiff Leo XIV and your Excellency, our Local Ordinary.

At the same time, after many years of discernment, the suppression of the Diocesan Traditional Latin mass, and the needs of the souls of the faithful of the Knoxville Diocese, we have reached the conscientious conviction that the present crisis in the Church, particularly as it concerns doctrine, liturgy, priestly life, and the salvation of souls, requires us to follow this course. We understand that you may judge our decision differently. We nevertheless make this decision in good faith and according to our consciences, believing that we remain bound to serve Christ and His Church faithfully as a Catholic priest and Catholic laity. For myself, I must also live in fidelity to my vocation as a son of Saint Francis. I am continuing to pursue my Capuchin/Franciscan vocation with a Traditional Capuchin Congregation.

I sincerely hope that, despite our disagreement concerning the course we have chosen, this matter can be handled peacefully and pastorally. We do not wish to engage in polemics or recriminations, nor do we wish unnecessarily to aggravate an already difficult situation. It is the hope of the Community that you would be so kind and accommodating as to give us your approval and that you would accept our invitation to bless our new Mission Chapel when it is completed. We certainly welcome your blessing and your support of our Mission.

Should you determine that any canonical or administrative measure affecting my status as a priest living in your Diocese or that of the members of the community are necessary, we respectfully ask that it be communicated to us formally in writing, with sufficient indication of its juridical basis, scope, and reasons, so that we may understand the measure and exercise the rights which the law of the Church affords us.

We are receiving canonical advice and will seek appropriate canonical assistance in responding to any formal measures that may arise. Nothing in this letter should be understood as a renunciation of any canonical rights or remedies available to myself as a priest or the lay faithful of our Mission and Foundation.

I remain grateful for the priesthood Our Lord has entrusted to me and remain committed to the salvation of souls and to fidelity to the Catholic faith. Please be assured of my continued prayers for you, and I sincerely ask that you remember us in yours as we move forward.

May Our Lady guide and protect us both.

Respectfully in Christ,\
Fr. David Mary of Our Lady of Sorrows\
Saint Francis of Assisi Mission Community\
Una Voce Tennessee Foundation
