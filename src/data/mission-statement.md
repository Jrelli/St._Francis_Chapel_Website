Saint Francis Mission Chapel is affiliated with the Society of Saint Pius X (S.S.P.X.) but the SSPX does not govern its operations and assumes no legal obligations on its behalf. The mission of Saint Francis Mission Chapel is committed to the primary Mission of the Holy Roman Catholic Church, the salvation of souls, and the preservation and dissemination of the One, Holy, Catholic, and Apostolic Faith.

While acknowledging the Roman Pontiff as the successor of Saint Peter as well as those ordinaries he has appointed, we also recognize the present crisis rooted in Modernism, Ecumenism, Collegiality, and Religious Liberty, which have infiltrated the Holy Roman Church and are experienced on the parish level in various ways, most particularly in the celebration of the Novus Ordo and the celebration of the other Sacraments.

Even though there have been many statements and proclamations from the Holy Father the Pope and various decrees against the Society of Saint Pius X, we remain members of the Holy Roman Catholic Church. Any decrees of excommunication or accusations of schism are invalid since we recognize and acknowledge the Roman Pontiff as the legitimate successor of Saint Peter and Pope of Rome, we do not seek to establish a parallel Church, and that we hold to the true and authentic traditional teachings of the Holy Roman Catholic Church. In order to protect, preserve, and perpetuate the fullness of the One True Faith in this time of crisis, we have no other option than to establish this Mission Chapel as a means to that end. When the crisis in the Church is over and the hierarchy of the Church has purified herself of these errors we shall happily submit to her authentically exercised authority.

Saint Francis Mission Chapel is serviced by the Traditional Capuchin Friars Minor, an independent Capuchin-Franciscan Community. Fr. David Mary is affiliated with the SSPX. The Traditional Capuchin Friars Minor live the authentic Traditional Capuchin Life according to their 800-year-old traditions and liturgical practices.

Saint Francis Mission Chapel is organized and supported by Una Voce Tennessee, a 501(c)(3) tax exempt non-profit entity created to help administer the everyday requirements of the chapel. Una Voce is an international federation of associations, founded in 1966 in Rome, that now includes national associations in 17 nations on every continent. It is dedicated to ensuring that the Roman Mass codified by St. Pius V is maintained as one of the forms of eucharistic worship which are honored in universal liturgical life, and to restoring the use of Latin, Gregorian Chant, and sacred polyphony in Catholic liturgy. The Una Voce International website provides information about officers and national associations: [www.fiuv.org](https://www.fiuv.org).

Saint Francis Mission Chapel functions as if it were a regular parish with a normal Sunday Mass and Confession Schedule, Holy Days of Obligation Schedule, and various devotions. We also intend to have various groups, Sacramental Preparation Classes, First Friday and First Saturday Devotions. Daily Mass and daily confessions are available at Holy Crucifixion Hermitage.

With recommendation and discernment, the priest will choose lay persons to staff the Mission Chapel. Roles such as secretary, business manager, sacristan, communications persons, as well as other important positions will be assigned by the priest.
