In October 2025, the Diocese of Knoxville decided to phase out the Traditional Latin Mass. The three parishes that offered it, in Chattanooga, Knoxville, and Johnson City, each set their own final date. At Holy Ghost in Knoxville, the last Traditional Latin Mass was offered on Christmas Day 2025.

The Catholics who had worshiped at those Masses responded in different ways. Some petitioned Bishop Beckman to restore the Mass in the parishes, or to invite the Priestly Fraternity of Saint Peter or the Institute of Christ the King. Others looked to the Society of Saint Pius X.

In 2026, a group of lay Catholics asked the Society for help. The Society had no priest to send, but the faithful could be served by a priest of their own who was willing to associate with it. Father David Mary of Our Lady of Sorrows, a Franciscan priest, agreed. His letter to Bishop Beckman, below, explains his decision in his own words.

In June 2026, the mission bought five acres of land in Jefferson County, Tennessee. On the property stands a large barn, which volunteers are converting into a chapel with a sanctuary, a community center, and classrooms. [Follow the chapel build](/chapel-build/).

Until the chapel is finished, Father David Mary offers a sung Traditional Latin Mass every Sunday, with Confession beforehand. Visitors are always welcome. [See Mass times](/).

The purpose of this mission is to save souls, and to bring the fullness of Catholic life to the Catholics of East Tennessee.
